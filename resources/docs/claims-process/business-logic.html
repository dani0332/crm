📋 Claim Lifecycle Overview

The complete claim lifecycle from creation to closure, with automated status transitions and LOB-specific business rules.

🔄 Complete Claim Workflow

1. Claim Creation

  • Internal policy search and validation
  • Customer information verification
  • LOB-specific field validation
  • CPAPI integration for claim creation only
  • Initial status: "New Claim"
  • Auto-assignment of sub-status based on LOB

2. Claim Registration

  • Claim number assignment triggers status update
  • Car/Bike: "Claim Registered Awaiting Inspection"
  • Health/Life: "Claim Registered"
  • Document collection and validation
  • Manager assignment and notification

3. Processing & Review

  • Under review by claims team
  • Additional documentation requests
  • Assessment and evaluation
  • Approval amount calculations (Car/Bike)
  • Service type validation (Health)

4. Decision & Settlement

  • Approval/denial decision
  • Automatic status updates based on amounts
  • Settlement processing
  • Customer notification with AI optimization
  • Document finalization

5. Closure

  • Automatic closure detection
  • Final status updates
  • Audit trail completion
  • Archive and reporting

🏢 Line of Business (LOB) Specific Rules

🚗 Motor/Car & Bike Claims

  • Required Fields: plate_number, car_make, car_model, model_year (all interdependent)
  • Validation Pattern: Plate number regex: /^[A-Za-z0-9\-\s]+$/
  • Year Range: 1900 to current year + 1
  • Initial Status: "Claim Registered Awaiting Inspection"
  • Approval Amounts: Three types trigger automatic status updates
  • Closure States: Repair settled, Total loss settled, Cash loss settled, Denied, Withdrawn
  • Shared Logic: Car and Bike use identical business rules

🏥 Health & Group Medical Claims

  • Request Types: Reimbursement, Pending Approvals, Ask a Question
  • Required Fields: claim_request_type_id, service_type_id
  • Conditional Validation: Service type required for pending approvals
  • Service Types: In-patient, Out-patient requests
  • Status Flow: Different flows based on request type
  • Closure States: Claim paid, Request approved, Answered & closed, Denied, Withdrawn
  • Group Medical: Corporate health insurance claims

💼 Life Claims

  • Simplified Flow: Basic claim processing with minimal sub-statuses
  • Status Management: Streamlined approval process
  • Closure States: Claim paid, Claim denied (simplified logic)
  • Processing: Direct path from registration to settlement
  • Validation: Standard claim validation without LOB-specific fields
  • Documentation: Standard life insurance claim documents

🏠 Other LOBs (Home, Travel, Marine, Pet, Business, etc.)

  • Standard Processing: Basic claim workflow for all other lines of business
  • Common Logic: Standard Open → Closed workflow with minimal sub-statuses
  • Documentation: Standard claim documents and evidence
  • Status Flow: Simplified processing without LOB-specific business rules
  • Closure States: Standard claim paid, denied, or withdrawn
  • Includes: Home, Travel, Marine (Yacht/Jetski), Pet, Cycle, Business, CorpLine, Group Medical, Savings, and other specialty products

🤖 Observer Pattern & Automated Business Logic

🎯 ClaimRequestObserver Monitoring

The ClaimRequestObserver automatically triggers business logic based on field changes, ensuring consistent workflow management across all claims.

Claim Number Entry Monitoring
  • Trigger: When claim_number field changes from empty to filled
  • Action: Auto-updates sub-status to "Claim Registered"
  • Method: updateClaimSubStatusToClaimRegistered()
  • LOB Specific: Car/Bike get "Awaiting Inspection" status
Sub-Status Change Monitoring
  • Trigger: When claim_sub_status_id field changes
  • Action: Evaluates closure logic based on LOB-specific rules
  • Method: checkSubStatusForClaimClosure()
  • Result: Automatic claim closure if closure state reached
Approval Amount Monitoring (Car/Bike Only)
  • Repair Amount: Triggers "Repair approved & work in progress"
  • Total Loss Amount: Triggers "Total Loss Offer Letter shared"
  • Cash Loss Amount: Triggers "Cash loss approved"
  • Condition: Only triggers when changing from empty to filled
Status Change Monitoring
  • Trigger: When claim_status_id field changes
  • Action: Audit logging and status tracking
  • Implementation: Comprehensive change tracking via OwenIt/Auditing
  • Logging: All status changes logged with user context

🔒 Closure Logic by Line of Business

Motor/Car & Bike Closure States

  • Repair Completed and Settled - Repair work finished and payment made
  • Total Loss Paid and Settled - Total loss settlement completed
  • Cash Loss Paid and Settled - Cash loss payment completed

  • Claim Withdrawn - Customer or system withdrawal

Health & Group Medical Closure States

  • Request Approved - Pending approval request approved

Life Insurance Closure States

  • Claim Paid - Life insurance benefit paid
  • Claim Denied - Life claim rejected

  • Direct Processing - Streamlined approval process

Other LOBs Closure States

  • Closure States: Claim paid, Claim denied, Claim withdrawn
  • Includes: Home, Travel, Marine, Pet, Cycle, Business, CorpLine, Group Medical, Savings, and other specialty products
  • Simplified Logic: No LOB-specific closure rules or complex status transitions
  • Processing: Standard claim workflow from registration to settlement

🔧 Validation Rules & Business Constraints

Car/Motor Validation Rules

  • Interdependent Fields: All car fields must be provided together
  • Plate Number: Regex pattern validation
  • Model Year: Range 1900 to current year + 1
  • Auto-Uppercase: Plate numbers converted to uppercase
  • Required Together: plate_number, car_make, car_model, model_year

Health Validation Rules

  • Request Type: Must be selected from valid options
  • Service Type: Required for pending approvals
  • Conditional Logic: Service type validation based on request type
  • Lookup Validation: Against lookups table
  • Reference Numbers: Optional but validated if provided

General Validation Rules

  • Name Validation: Regex pattern for first/last names
  • Phone Validation: International phone number format
  • Email Validation: Standard email format, lowercase conversion
  • Date Validation: Incident date must be before today
  • Amount Validation: Positive decimal values for financial fields

Business Constraints

  • Permission Checks: Role-based access control
  • Status Transitions: Valid state machine enforcement
  • Document Requirements: LOB-specific document types
  • Manager Assignment: Required for processing
  • Audit Trail: All changes logged with user context
