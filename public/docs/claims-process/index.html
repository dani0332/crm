🏢 Line of Business (LOB) Specific Rules

Each Line of Business has unique validation rules, status transitions, and business logic requirements.

🚗 Motor/Car & Bike Claims

  • Required Fields: Plate number, car make, model, year (all interdependent)
  • Validation: Plate number regex pattern, year range validation
  • Status Flow: Awaiting Inspection → Repair/Total Loss/Cash Loss paths
  • Approval Amounts: Three types trigger automatic status updates
  • Closure States: Settled, Denied, Withdrawn
  • Shared Logic: Car and Bike use identical business rules

🏥 Health & Group Medical Claims

  • Request Types: Reimbursement, Pending Approvals, Ask a Question
  • Service Types: In-patient, Out-patient requests
  • Conditional Validation: Service type required for pending approvals
  • Status Flow: Different flows based on request type
  • Closure States: Paid, Approved, Answered & Closed, Denied
  • Group Medical: Corporate health insurance claims

💼 Life Claims

  • Simplified Flow: Basic claim processing with minimal sub-statuses
  • Status Management: Streamlined approval process
  • Closure States: Paid, Denied (simplified logic)
  • Processing: Direct path from registration to settlement
  • Validation: Standard claim validation without LOB-specific fields

🏠 Other LOBs (Home, Travel, Marine, Pet, Business, etc.)

  • Standard Processing: Basic claim workflow for all other lines of business
  • Common Logic: Standard Open → Closed workflow with minimal sub-statuses
  • Documentation: Standard claim documents and evidence
  • Status Flow: Simplified processing without LOB-specific business rules
  • Closure States: Standard claim paid, denied, or withdrawn
  • Includes: Home, Travel, Marine (Yacht/Jetski), Pet, Cycle, Business, CorpLine, Group Medical, Savings, and other specialty products

🔄 Status Transition & Observer Logic

Automatic business logic triggers based on field changes using Laravel Observer pattern.

🎯 Monitored Field Changes

Claim Number Entry
  • Trigger: When claim_number changes from empty to filled
  • Action: Auto-updates sub-status to "Claim Registered"
  • Car/Bike: Updates to "Claim Registered Awaiting Inspection"
  • Health/Life: Updates to "Claim Registered"
Sub-Status Changes
  • Trigger: When claim_sub_status_id field changes
  • Action: Evaluates closure logic based on LOB-specific rules
  • Result: Automatic claim closure if closure state reached
  • Logging: All status transitions logged with user context
Repair Amount (Car/Bike Only)
  • Trigger: When approved_repair_amount changes from empty to filled
  • Action: Auto-updates to "Repair approved & work in progress"
  • Condition: Only triggers for Car and Bike LOBs
  • Business Logic: Indicates repair work has been approved
Total Loss Amount (Car/Bike Only)
  • Trigger: When approved_total_loss_amount changes from empty to filled
  • Action: Auto-updates to "Total Loss Offer Letter shared"
  • Condition: Only triggers for Car and Bike LOBs
  • Business Logic: Indicates vehicle is declared total loss
Cash Loss Amount (Car/Bike Only)
  • Trigger: When approved_cash_loss_amount changes from empty to filled
  • Action: Auto-updates to "Cash loss approved"
  • Condition: Only triggers for Car and Bike LOBs
  • Business Logic: Indicates cash settlement has been approved
Status Changes
  • Trigger: When claim_status_id field changes
  • Action: Prepared for Google review email dispatch
  • Status: Currently commented out, ready for activation
  • Integration: SendGoogleReviewEmailJob prepared

🔒 Closure Logic by LOB

Motor/Car & Bike Closure States
  • Repair Completed and Settled - Repair work finished and payment made
  • Total Loss Paid and Settled - Total loss settlement completed
  • Cash Loss Paid and Settled - Cash loss payment completed
  • Claim Denied - Claim rejected after review
  • Claim Withdrawn - Customer or system withdrawal
Health & Group Medical Closure States
  • Claim Paid - Reimbursement payment completed
  • Request Approved - Pending approval request approved
  • Answered and Closed - Ask a question request answered
  • Request Denied - Health request rejected
  • Request Withdrawn - Customer withdrawal
Life Insurance Closure States
  • Claim Paid - Life insurance benefit paid
  • Claim Denied - Life claim rejected
  • Simplified Logic - Minimal sub-status transitions
Other LOBs Closure States
  • Claim Paid - Standard settlement completed
  • Claim Denied - Standard claim rejection
  • Claim Withdrawn - Customer or system withdrawal
  • Standard Logic - No LOB-specific closure rules
