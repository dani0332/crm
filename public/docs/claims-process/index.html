🏢 Line of Business (LOB) Specific Rules

Each Line of Business has unique validation rules, status transitions, and business logic requirements.

🚗 Motor/Car & Bike Claims

  • Required Fields: Plate number, car make, model, year (all interdependent)
  • Validation: Plate number regex pattern, year range validation
  • Status Flow: Awaiting Inspection → Repair/Total Loss/Cash Loss paths
  • Approval Amounts: Three types trigger automatic status updates
  • Closure States: Settled, Denied, Withdrawn
  • Shared Logic: Car and Bike use identical business rules

🏥 Health & Group Medical Claims

  • Request Types: Reimbursement, Pending Approvals, Ask a Question
  • Service Types: In-patient, Out-patient requests
  • Conditional Validation: Service type required for pending approvals
  • Status Flow: Different flows based on request type
  • Closure States: Paid, Approved, Answered & Closed, Denied
  • Group Medical: Corporate health insurance claims

💼 Life Claims

  • Simplified Flow: Basic claim processing with minimal sub-statuses
  • Status Management: Streamlined approval process
  • Closure States: Paid, Denied (simplified logic)
  • Processing: Direct path from registration to settlement
  • Validation: Standard claim validation without LOB-specific fields

🏠 Home & Property Claims

  • Standard Processing: Basic claim workflow
  • Property Details: Location and damage assessment
  • Document Types: Property photos, damage reports
  • Status Flow: Standard Open → Closed workflow

✈️ Travel Claims

  • Trip Details: Travel dates and destinations
  • Claim Types: Medical, cancellation, baggage, delay
  • Documentation: Receipts, medical reports, airline confirmations
  • Processing: Standard claim workflow

⛵ Marine (Yacht, Jetski) Claims

  • Vessel Information: Registration, specifications
  • Incident Types: Collision, theft, weather damage
  • Survey Requirements: Marine surveyor reports
  • Processing: Specialized marine claim workflow

🐕 Pet & Specialty Claims

  • Pet Claims: Veterinary expenses and treatments
  • Cycle Claims: Bicycle theft and damage
  • Specialty LOBs: Business, CorpLine, Savings products
  • Processing: Tailored workflows per product type

🔄 Status Transition & Observer Logic

Automatic business logic triggers based on field changes using Laravel Observer pattern.

🎯 Monitored Field Changes

Claim Number Entry Triggers sub-status update to "Claim Registered"
Sub-Status Changes Evaluates closure logic based on LOB-specific rules
Repair Amount Car/Bike: Auto-updates to "Repair approved & work in progress"
Total Loss Amount Car/Bike: Auto-updates to "Total Loss Offer Letter shared"
Cash Loss Amount Car/Bike: Auto-updates to "Cash loss approved"
Status Changes Prepared for Google review email dispatch integration

🔒 Closure Logic by LOB

Motor/Car Closure Repair settled, Total loss settled, Cash loss settled, Denied, Withdrawn
Health Closure Claim paid, Request approved, Answered & closed, Denied, Withdrawn
Life Closure Claim paid, Claim denied (simplified logic)
Automatic Updates Observer triggers status changes and logs all transitions
